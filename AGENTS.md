# Copy: no AI slop

Every screen, page, card and chat line uses as few words as possible.

- No subtitles. A title is followed by the thing to do (a button, a field, the content), never by a sentence restating or selling it ("Get started with Noni in a minute.", "Sign in to your Noni account.", "Everything you need, all in one place." are all banned).
- Titles are 2 to 4 plain words. Buttons say the action.
- No helper or reassurance lines ("It only takes a minute", "We'll handle the rest", "No credit card required") unless the owner asked for that exact line.
- Noni speaks as software that does things automatically, never as "we do it for you".
- No dashes as punctuation, no emoji, no hype words.

When in doubt, delete the line.
